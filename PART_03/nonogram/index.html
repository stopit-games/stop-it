STAGE CLEAR!

How to play

The numbers on the left describe each ROW. The numbers above describe each COLUMN. Each number is the length of one continuous block of filled cells. Separate blocks with at least one empty cell.

2 · 1■■×■×××

Example: 2 · 1 means a block of 2 and a block of 1, with at least one gap. This is one possible arrangement; the other clues decide where the blocks go.

1. Look for a row with a large number. A 7 in a 7-cell row fills that whole row. 2. Compare the column clues to work out the next cells. 3. Use Fill for filled cells and × Mark for cells you know are empty. Undo reverses a tap. Hint fills one logically certain cell and explains why.
