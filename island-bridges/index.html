Connect the islands

Each number is the total number of bridges touching that island. Connect every island into one network.

①

Tap one island, then another in the same row or column. No island may lie between them.

②

Repeat the same pair: 1 bridge → 2 bridges → no bridge. Bridges cannot cross. Dragging is not needed.

③

A green check means the number matches. Checked islands can still be changed. Undo reverses one move.

All numbers must match AND all islands must connect. Loops are allowed. No time limit.
