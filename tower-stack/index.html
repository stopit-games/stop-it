How to play & stacking tips

Stop each moving circle as it overlaps the top of your tower and stack as high as you can. There is no fixed clear height; your score is the number of circles you stack.

Choose a mode and press START GAME. Tap or click the playfield, or press Space, to stop the moving circle when it overlaps the target. Each circle makes only one pass.

The game ends if you stop with too little overlap or let the circle pass the target. Stop closer to the center to keep more size for the next circle. A centered PERFECT preserves the circle’s size.

Circles first approach from four directions. From the sixth circle, diagonals join for eight directions. They speed up as you stack, so watch the approach direction and align the centers.

CLASSIC uses a top-down view to help you align positions. 3D CHALLENGE shows your tower from an angle as it grows. Both modes challenge you to time the overlap.

After a run, review your tower and select PLAY AGAIN to start from zero. Each mode saves its own best score in this browser. Toggle music from the gear settings.
